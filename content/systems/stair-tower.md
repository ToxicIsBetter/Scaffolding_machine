## When to use it

Ladders are a last resort for access on large or long-running scaffolds. Stair towers (usually built in a [[multidirectional-system]]) carry people and hand-held tools safely, and can double as an emergency escape route.

## Key requirements

- Built to the manufacturer's configuration, tied as the manufacturer specifies.
- Handrails on both sides, with gates at each landing onto working lifts.
- Include stair towers in the fire and emergency plan.

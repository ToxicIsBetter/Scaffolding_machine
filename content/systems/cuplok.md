## When to use it

Cuplok uses fixed bottom cups and sliding top cups at fixed intervals on the standards. Ledger blades are locked in by a single hammer blow on the top cup. It's strong and quick, but its fixed node spacing makes it less flexible than [[tube-and-fitting]].

## Key requirements

- Erect to the manufacturer's guidance. Bespoke configurations and all falsework need a design under [[temporary-works]].
- Lift heights are fixed by the cup spacing on the standards.

## Common pitfalls

- Top cups not fully rotated and hammered home - check them at every inspection.
- Damaged blades or cups put back into use.

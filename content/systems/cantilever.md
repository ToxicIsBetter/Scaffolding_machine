## When to use it

When you can't stand a scaffold on the ground - a busy pavement, a lower roof that can't take load, or work that's only needed on the upper floors. The scaffold is supported on **cantilevered beams or needles** passing through openings or fixed to the structure.

## Key requirements

- **Always a bespoke design** - raise a [[design-brief-checklist]].
- The design must check the building can carry the reactions: floor slabs, window openings, and the counterweight or back-propping.
- Fixings and anchors tested per [[tg4-anchors]]. Designs go through the client's [[temporary-works]] procedure.
- Usually needs a [[protection-fan]] or netting beneath if the public are below.

## Common pitfalls

- Back-props inside the building removed by fit-out trades - label them clearly.
- Window openings used for needles get replaced during the works.

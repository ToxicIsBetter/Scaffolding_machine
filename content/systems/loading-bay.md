## When to use it

Whenever materials are landed onto the scaffold. Loading bays are built to a higher load class than the access scaffold ([[load-classes]]). TG20 compliance sheets cover some standard loading-bay configurations - otherwise, design one.

## Key requirements

- Safe working load clearly signed on the bay, and the same figure on the handover certificate ([[handover-procedure]]).
- Loading bay gates (up-and-over or sliding) so the edge is never left open.
- Additional ties and bracing around the bay as designed.

## Common pitfalls

- Pallets landed on the main scaffold instead of the bay.
- Gates left open or removed.

## When to use it

When there's no ground or floor to build up from: over water, roads or voids. The scaffold hangs from the structure above on tubes, chains or beams.

> **Warning:** Suspended scaffolds are high-consequence. Always a bespoke design, always led by an Advanced Scaffolder ([[cisrs-competence]]), and always with a rescue plan.

## Key requirements

- Bespoke design, including the load paths into the supporting structure, and the method and sequence of erection.
- Check suspension points and fixings ([[tg4-anchors]]).
- Debris netting or a sealed deck to protect whatever is below - especially roads, railways and water.
- Third-party approvals where they apply (e.g. Network Rail or highway authority possessions).

## Common pitfalls

- Erection sequence not planned - the scaffold is most vulnerable while partly built.

## When to use it

For short-duration work where a full scaffold isn't justified - internal maintenance, small repairs, inspections. On firm, level ground only.

## Key requirements

- PASMA-trained assemblers, using 3T or AGR methods ([[mobile-towers-en1004]]).
- Maximum heights and outrigger requirements are set by the manufacturer's manual - follow them.
- Castors locked whenever the tower is in use.

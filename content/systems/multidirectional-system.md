## When to use it

Multidirectional systems (for example Layher Allround or PERI UP-type systems) have a rosette or ring at regular intervals on each standard, so ledgers and braces can connect at many angles. They're quick to erect on large, repetitive elevations, and they're the usual base for [[temporary-roof]] and [[stair-tower]] structures.

## Key requirements

- Erect in line with the **manufacturer's instructions for assembly and use** - TG20 does not cover system scaffolds.
- Configurations outside the manufacturer's standard ones need a bespoke design ([[design-brief-checklist]]).
- Operatives need manufacturer product training as well as their CISRS card ([[cisrs-competence]]).
- Never mix components from different manufacturers' systems.

## Common pitfalls

- Using look-alike components from another system - they're not interchangeable, even when they fit.
- Missing wedge checks during weekly inspection.

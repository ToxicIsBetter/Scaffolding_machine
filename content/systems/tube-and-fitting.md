## When to use it

Choose tube and fitting when the building is irregular, the scaffold has to work around obstructions, or the job needs a lot of bespoke detailing. It's slower to erect than a [[multidirectional-system]], but it can be adapted to almost anything.

## Components

| Component | Standard |
|---|---|
| Steel scaffold tube (48.3 mm OD) | BS EN 39 |
| Couplers (right-angle, swivel, sleeve, putlog) | BS EN 74 |
| Timber scaffold boards | BS 2482 |
| Base plates and sole boards | Per design / TG20 |

## Key requirements

- Check the configuration against a [[tg20]] compliance sheet. If it's not covered, request a design ([[design-brief-checklist]]).
- Ties to the pattern on the compliance sheet, and anchors tested to [[tg4-anchors]].
- Erection to the [[sg4]] method; handover via [[handover-procedure]].

## Common pitfalls

- Sheeting added after handover without checking wind loading and ties - this changes the design.
- Ties removed by other trades for window or render works.
- Mixed tube types (steel and aluminium) on one scaffold.

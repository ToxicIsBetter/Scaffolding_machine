## When to use it

Re-roofing or structural work where the building must stay watertight - schools, occupied homes, heritage buildings. Usually a [[multidirectional-system]] roof (for example a cassette or keder-sheet roof) on top of a supporting scaffold.

## Key requirements

- **Always a bespoke design.** Sheeted roofs attract large wind and uplift loads, so the design must cover ties, ballast and the supporting scaffold.
- Plan the sheeting and roof erection sequence for the weather - most systems are rolled or craned into place.
- Consider snow loading, rainwater discharge (gutters and downpipes), and lighting under the roof.

## Common pitfalls

- Sheeting left loose before forecast high winds - have a high-wind check procedure.
- Downpipes discharging onto the public highway or neighbours.

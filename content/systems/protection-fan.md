## When to use it

Where people pass below or alongside the scaffold - footways, building entrances, neighbouring yards. Often a condition of the highway licence ([[highway-licence]]).

## Key requirements

- Fans are boarded and sheeted, projecting and angled up to catch debris. Check their size and position against TG20 or the design.
- Pedestrian gantries need a solid deck, side protection, lighting and enough headroom.
- Clear the debris from fans at every inspection.

## Common pitfalls

- Fans overloaded with debris that was never cleared.
- Gantry lighting failing at night - make it part of the weekly inspection.

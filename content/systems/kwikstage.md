## When to use it

Kwikstage uses V-pressings on the standards with wedge-ended ledgers and transoms, and steel battens for platforms. It's quick to erect on repetitive low and mid-rise housing elevations.

## Key requirements

- Erect to the manufacturer's guidance and standard configurations.
- Check every wedge is driven home. Use steel battens with locking where the system specifies them.

## Common pitfalls

- Mixing Kwikstage-pattern components from different suppliers that don't share the same tolerances.

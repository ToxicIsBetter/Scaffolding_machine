## When to use it

When you need a large, full working deck at high level inside a building - for ceilings, plasterwork, lighting, roof soffits. Simple birdcages may fall within [[tg20]] guidance; tall or heavily loaded ones need a design.

## Key requirements

- **Check the floor first.** The whole scaffold load goes into the slab or ground underneath - get the floor capacity from the client or engineer.
- Bracing in both directions, plus ties or buttresses to the structure where available.
- Edge protection at the deck perimeter and around any openings.

## Common pitfalls

- Suspended floors or basements under the scaffold that nobody checked.
- Ladder access left unsecured, or no stair tower provided for a big workforce.

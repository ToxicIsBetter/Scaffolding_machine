## Scope

Stone repairs and cleaning to the upper floors of an occupied office building. The pavement below is a major pedestrian route, so the City would only allow a minimal footprint.

## Scaffold solution

- A [[cantilever]] scaffold on steel beams through level-5 windows, back-propped inside the offices.
- [[tube-and-fitting]] above, to work around the stone cornices.
- A [[protection-fan]] at level 5, plus netting to the full height.

## Lessons learned

- Back-props sat inside occupied offices. A tenant liaison plan and labelled "DO NOT REMOVE" props were essential.
- TG4 anchor tests on the old stone showed variable results. The design was revised to through-ties at the window heads.

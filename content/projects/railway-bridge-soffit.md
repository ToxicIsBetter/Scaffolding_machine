## Scope

Access to the whole brick arch soffit for repointing and repairs. The road below must stay open, apart from short overnight closures.

## Proposed solution

- [[suspended]] scaffold hung from the bridge parapets, erected during road closures.
- A sealed deck to protect road users below.

## Open questions for the tender

- Rail asset owner's approvals and possession windows.
- Road closure licence lead time (borough).
- Can the parapet fixings be accepted? Needs [[tg4-anchors]] testing on a sample.

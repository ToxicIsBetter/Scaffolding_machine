## Scope

Replace the whole flat and pitched roof of a Victorian school building during the 7-week summer holiday.

## Scaffold solution

- [[multidirectional-system]] perimeter scaffold with a craned cassette [[temporary-roof]].
- A [[stair-tower]] in place of ladders, because more than 20 operatives were working at peak.

## Lessons learned

- The roof went on in 2 days because we pre-assembled the roof sections in the playground. Book the crane early.
- Strike had to finish before the term started, with no slippage possible. Programme a 3-day buffer.
- DBS and safeguarding requirements for site staff applied even with the school empty.

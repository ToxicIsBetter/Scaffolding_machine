## Scope

Access for brickwork, cladding and window installation on all four elevations of a 16-storey new-build residential block beside the river.

## Scaffold solution

- [[multidirectional-system]] scaffold, designed and checked as Category 2 temporary works under the principal contractor's [[temporary-works]] procedure.
- Two [[stair-tower]]s for trade access and emergency escape.
- Crane-served [[loading-bay]] on each elevation every 4th lift.
- [[protection-fan]] at level 2 over the riverside walkway.

## Key documents

- DES-2026-008 rev F (scaffold and loading bays)
- Permit to load from the principal contractor's TWC for each phase
- Weekly inspection reports - in the site register

## Lessons learned (so far)

- Phased handovers by elevation and lift kept the brickwork gang moving. Agree the handover zones at the start.
- High exposure by the river: the netting design was reviewed for wind, and extra ties were added at the corners.

## Scope

Full re-roof of six Victorian terraced houses in a conservation area. The residents stayed in their homes throughout, so the roofs had to stay watertight at all times.

## Scaffold solution

- Front and rear [[independent-tied]] scaffolds in [[tube-and-fitting]]. The varying bay windows and rear outriggers made a system scaffold impractical.
- A [[temporary-roof]] spanning the whole terrace, designed for sheeted wind loading (DES-2025-031).
- A [[loading-bay]] at the gable end for slates and timber, with a [[protection-fan]] over the footway.

## Key documents

- Design drawings and calculations: DES-2025-031 rev C
- Highway licence (s.169) from the borough
- Handover certificates HC-0412 to HC-0419
- Tie test records (TG4) - 3 visits

## Lessons learned

- **The highway licence took 6 weeks, not the 3 we programmed.** For this borough, apply at tender award.
- Conservation officer restricted drilling of the brickwork. Use reveal ties through window openings, and agree the tie locations with the conservation officer before design.
- Residents' parking suspensions needed 10 working days' notice for crane days.

## Scope

Replace the roof liner and insulation from below, in three phases, while the client keeps operating in the other two-thirds of the warehouse.

## Scaffold solution

- [[birdcage]] in [[cuplok]], moved bay by bay in three phases.
- [[mobile-access-tower]]s for the snagging on each phase.

## Lessons learned (so far)

- The slab capacity was confirmed by the client's engineer before design. Ask for it at enquiry stage.
- The forklift routes next to the birdcage needed impact barriers.

## What it covers

Erecting a scaffold on the public highway needs a **licence from the highway authority** under section 169 of the Highways Act 1980. In London, that's usually the borough council, or Transport for London for red routes.

## Checklist before erecting

- [ ] Licence applied for in good time (lead times vary by borough - check the project page for past experience)
- [ ] Public liability insurance certificate attached to the application
- [ ] Scaffold drawing showing footway width left clear for pedestrians
- [ ] Licence displayed on the scaffold
- [ ] Lighting at night on corners and projections, as the licence requires
- [ ] Standards at pavement level padded/marked; protection fans or gantry if the public pass beneath ([[protection-fan]])
- [ ] Parking suspensions booked for delivery and strike days

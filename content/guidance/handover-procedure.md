## Steps

1. Supervisor completes the scaffold and carries out a self-check against the drawing or TG20 compliance sheet.
2. A competent inspector carries out the **handover inspection** ([[inspection-regime]]).
3. The handover certificate is issued, signed by us and countersigned by the client's representative.
4. The scaffold tag is set to green and the scaffold is entered on the site scaffold register.

## The handover certificate must state

- Scaffold location and extent (grid lines / elevation)
- Drawing number or TG20 compliance sheet reference
- **Load class** ([[load-classes]]) and maximum number of loaded lifts
- Permitted use (e.g. "brickwork access - no material storage on top lift")
- Tie arrangement, and that ties must not be removed without our permission
- Who to contact for alterations - **only our scaffolders may alter the scaffold**

> **Warning:** Partial handovers must clearly mark the areas not handed over with barriers and red tags.

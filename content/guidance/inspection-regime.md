## When to inspect

Under [[work-at-height-regs]] Regulation 12, a scaffold from which a person could fall 2 m or more must be inspected:

1. after installation and **before first use** (the handover inspection);
2. at intervals of **no more than 7 days**;
3. after any event liable to affect its safety - high winds, alteration, damage, ties removed, impact.

## Who inspects

A competent person - for our scaffolds, a holder of a CISRS Scaffold Inspection qualification appropriate to the complexity of the scaffold ([[cisrs-competence]]).

## The report

- Complete the report **before the end of the working period**, and give it to the person it was done for within 24 hours.
- Include the particulars listed in WAHR Schedule 7 (location, description, date and time, defects and risks found, action taken, name and position of the inspector).
- Keep the report on site until the work is complete, then at the office for at least 3 months.

## On-site checklist

- [ ] Tag updated (green = safe to use, red = do not use) and dated
- [ ] Ties present and as per design / compliance sheet
- [ ] Guardrails, toe boards and brick guards complete
- [ ] Platforms fully boarded, no trip hazards or overloading
- [ ] Base plates, sole boards and ground conditions sound
- [ ] Sheeting / netting secure; no unplanned wind loading
- [ ] Access ladders/stairs secured and gates closing

## What it covers

BS 5975 is the code of practice for temporary works procedures. Designed scaffolds are temporary works, so they go through the principal contractor's procedure.

## Key roles

- **Temporary Works Coordinator (TWC)** - appointed by the principal contractor; registers the scaffold, makes sure the design is checked, and issues the permit to load.
- **Temporary Works Supervisor (TWS)** - may be appointed to help the TWC on site.
- **Designer and design checker** - the independence of the checker depends on the design check category (0 to 3) assigned to the scaffold.

## What it means for us

- Every designed scaffold gets an entry on the client's temporary works register - ask for it at the pre-start meeting.
- Send drawings and calculations with enough time for the design check.
- No loading until the TWC has issued a **permit to load**.

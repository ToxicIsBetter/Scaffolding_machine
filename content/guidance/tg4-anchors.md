## What it covers

Ties are what keep an independent scaffold standing. TG4 covers choosing anchor types for the substrate (brick, block, concrete, stone), installing them, and **proof-testing** a sample of them on site.

## What it means on site

- Tie type and pattern come from the TG20 compliance sheet or the bespoke design - never reduce ties without design approval.
- Anchors are tested by a trained tester using a calibrated tester, in line with the test proportion and test load set out in TG4. Record the results on the tie test record and keep them in the job file.
- A failed anchor means the substrate needs investigating - stop and contact the Design Office.
- Ties removed for façade work must be replaced or substituted *before* the scaffold is used again, and the change recorded in the scaffold register.

> **Important:** Check the current edition of TG4 for test proportions and proof loads - do not rely on memory or old paperwork.

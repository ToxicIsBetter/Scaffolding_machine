## What it covers

BS EN 1004 covers the design of mobile access towers. PASMA (the Prefabricated Access Suppliers' and Manufacturers' Association) provides the recognised training and the two safe assembly methods: **3T (Through The Trap)** and **AGR (Advance Guard Rail)**.

## What it means on site

- Only PASMA-trained operatives assemble towers, following the manufacturer's instruction manual.
- Towers are inspected after assembly and every 7 days in the same way as scaffolds ([[inspection-regime]]).
- Outriggers/stabilisers are always fitted as the manual specifies; never move a tower with people or materials on it.
- See system page: [[mobile-access-tower]].

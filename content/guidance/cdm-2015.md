## What it covers

CDM 2015 sets out the duty holders on a construction project: client, principal designer, principal contractor, designers and contractors. On most jobs we are a **contractor** to the principal contractor, and our scaffold designers are **designers** under CDM.

## What it means for us

- We must plan, manage and monitor our own work and co-operate with the principal contractor's construction phase plan.
- Scaffold designs are temporary works designs - they sit within the principal contractor's temporary works procedure ([[temporary-works]]).
- Our RAMS (risk assessments and method statements) and scaffold drawings should be submitted to the principal contractor before work starts.
- Information about the scaffold (load class, permitted use, inspection regime) must reach the people who will use it - via the handover certificate ([[handover-procedure]]).

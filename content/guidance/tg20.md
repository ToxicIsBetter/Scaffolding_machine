## What it covers

TG20 is the National Access & Scaffolding Confederation's good-practice guidance for **tube and fitting** scaffolding. The current edition is TG20:21, made up of an operational guide, a design guide and the TG20 eGuide, which generates compliance sheets.

## How we use it

1. For each scaffold, check whether its configuration (height, bay length, lift height, duty/load class, sheeting, wind exposure, tie pattern) is covered by a TG20 compliance sheet.
2. **If it is covered:** print the compliance sheet, keep it in the job file and on site, and quote its reference on the handover certificate.
3. **If it is not covered:** it needs a bespoke design from a competent scaffold designer - raise a design brief ([[design-brief-checklist]]).

> **Important:** Common reasons a scaffold falls outside TG20: sheeting or debris netting at high wind exposure, loading bays outside the tabled limits, cantilevers, suspended scaffolds, temporary roofs, and heights beyond the tables.

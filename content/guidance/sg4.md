## What it covers

SG4 is the NASC's guidance on preventing falls during scaffolding operations. It follows the WAHR hierarchy ([[work-at-height-regs]]): **avoid** working at height where possible, **prevent** falls using collective measures, and only then **minimise** consequences with personal fall protection.

## What it means on site

- Use an **advance guardrail system** or step-up/advance guardrail methods so the scaffolder is protected before stepping onto a new lift.
- Where personal fall protection is still needed, scaffolders clip on in line with the SG4 sequence, and a **rescue plan** must be in place before work starts.
- Full boarded working platforms and the "scaffolder's safe zone" are planned into the erection sequence in the method statement.
- Supervisors brief the SG4 method at the start of each job and record it on the toolbox talk sheet.

> **Warning:** A harness without a rescue plan is not a compliant system of work.

## What it covers

The Construction Industry Scaffolders Record Scheme (CISRS) is the recognised training and card scheme for UK scaffolders. Card levels include labourer, trainee scaffolder, scaffolder, advanced scaffolder and supervisor, plus separate scaffold inspection qualifications.

## Our rules (sample)

| Task | Minimum competence |
|---|---|
| Labouring / handling materials | CISRS Labourer |
| Erecting basic tube and fitting / TG20 scaffolds | CISRS Scaffolder (trainees under direct supervision) |
| Complex or designed scaffolds (cantilevers, suspended, temporary roofs) | CISRS Advanced Scaffolder leading the gang |
| System scaffolds | Scaffolder plus manufacturer product training for that system |
| Weekly inspections | CISRS Scaffold Inspection qualification for the scaffold type |
| Running a site | CISRS Supervisor |

> **Important:** Check card validity on the CISRS website before allocating operatives - cards expire and must be renewed.

## Use this when

The scaffold is outside [[tg20]] compliance, or is a special scaffold (cantilever, suspended, temporary roof, large birdcage, loading tower).

## Brief checklist

- [ ] Site address, postcode and elevations affected (photos and existing drawings)
- [ ] Purpose of the scaffold and trades using it
- [ ] Required **load class** ([[load-classes]]) and number of working lifts
- [ ] Heights, platform widths, lift heights, any bridging or openings
- [ ] Sheeting / debris netting / temporary roof required? (drives wind loading)
- [ ] Ground conditions or supporting structure (slab, roof, basement, pavement vaults)
- [ ] Tie availability - façade material, openings, restrictions on drilling (listed buildings)
- [ ] Loading bays, hoists, rubbish chutes, stair access
- [ ] Public interface - highway, fans, gantries ([[highway-licence]])
- [ ] Programme: erect date, phased handovers, strike date
- [ ] Client's temporary works design check category ([[temporary-works]])

> **Important:** Search the hub for similar past projects first - reusing a proven design saves days.

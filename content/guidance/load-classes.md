## What it covers

BS EN 12811-1 sets performance requirements for working scaffolds, including six **load classes** for the platform's uniformly distributed load.

| Load class | Uniformly distributed load | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.75 kN/m² | Inspection only, light tools, no materials |
| 2 | 1.5 kN/m² | Light work - painting, cleaning, glazing |
| 3 | 2.0 kN/m² | General purpose - most building work |
| 4 | 3.0 kN/m² | Heavy duty - blockwork, brickwork |
| 5 | 4.5 kN/m² | Masonry / heavy materials storage |
| 6 | 6.0 kN/m² | Very heavy loads - designed loading areas |

## What it means on site

- The load class is agreed with the client **before** design, and appears on the drawing, the handover certificate and the scaffold tag.
- Older paperwork may use BS 5973 "duty" names (light, general purpose, heavy). Always convert to the EN load class when re-issuing.
- Loading bays and landing platforms usually need a higher class than the main scaffold - see [[loading-bay]].

> **Important:** Values above are as commonly quoted from BS EN 12811-1. Confirm against the purchased standard before using them in a design.
